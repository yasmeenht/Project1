About Me

01

Where It All Started

My journey into technology began with studying Computer Science at Yarmouk University. What first started as learning how computers and programs work gradually became an interest in how ideas can be transformed into things people can actually interact with. Along the way, I found myself enjoying the combination of logic, creativity, and problem solving that comes with development.

02

Finding My direction

Computer Science introduced me to many different areas of technology, but I wanted to become more confident in actually building things from the ground up. That curiosity eventually pulled me toward full-stack development, where I could understand not only what users see, but also what happens behind the scenes to make everything work together.

03

Learning to Think Like a Developer

For me, becoming a developer isn't just about learning another language or framework. I'm learning how to approach unfamiliar problems, break them into smaller pieces, experiment, make mistakes, and figure out why something works instead of simply making it work. I'm still developing that confidence, but that's also one of the parts of this journey I value most.
